Anime

Why I Like Anime

Anime is one of my favorite forms of entertainment. I started watching anime in 2020 and since then I have watched many different shows. I like anime because of the stories, characters, animation, and how different each show can be.

My Favorite Anime: Attack on Titan

Attack on Titan is my favorite anime. I love the story because it has soooo many plot twists, action, and amazing animation. As the story progresses, you learn more about the characters and the world they live in. I also like how the story becomes more complicated. It really also made me question the main character and whether he was right or wrong. It was a challenging watch for sure and I still think about it to this day. I constantly try to get my friends to watch it!
